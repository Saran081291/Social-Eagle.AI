# 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Meal Plan for Healthy Weight Loss
### Designed for a desk-job working professional in Tamil Nadu

---

## How to Use This Plan

- Portions are approximate. Use your palm/cup as a guide: 1 cup = 200 ml (a normal tea cup); 1 palm-size = roughly 80–100 g.
- Rice portions are kept moderate (¾–1 cup cooked) since your work involves long sitting hours with limited movement.
- Reduce oil to 1 tsp per dish where possible. Use gingelly (sesame) or groundnut oil in rotation.
- Curd, buttermilk, and sambar appear often — they are affordable, filling, and easy to digest.
- Swap any vegetable/dal for what is fresh and cheap in your local market that week — the *pattern* matters more than the exact vegetable.

## Daily Water Intake Guidance

- Target **2.5 to 3 litres** of water per day (about 10–12 glasses).
- Start the day with 1 glass of warm water.
- Drink 1 glass of water 30 minutes before each meal — this helps control portion size naturally.
- Carry a water bottle to your desk; refill it 3–4 times through the workday.
- Limit tea/coffee to 2 cups a day, and avoid adding excess sugar.

---

## Healthy Office Snacks (Keep at Your Desk)

- Roasted chana, peanuts, or makhana (small packets, portion-controlled)
- Fresh fruit (banana, apple, guava, orange) — easy to carry, no prep
- Buttermilk in a small flask
- Roasted foxnuts or puffed rice (murmura) with a pinch of chaat masala
- Cucumber or carrot sticks, if you can carry a small box

Avoid keeping biscuits, chips, or sugary snacks within easy reach at your desk.

---

## Desk-Work Movement Suggestions

- Stand up and stretch for 2–3 minutes every hour (set a phone reminder).
- Take a 10-minute walk after lunch and after dinner — this alone helps with weight management.
- Use stairs instead of the lift where possible.
- Do simple chair stretches (neck rolls, shoulder rolls, ankle rotations) between tasks.
- Try a short 15–20 minute walk or basic home exercise in the morning or evening, even 3–4 days a week.

---

## Tips to Reduce Sugary Drinks, Fried Foods, and Excess Portions

- Replace sugary tea/coffee and soft drinks with buttermilk, lemon water, or plain water.
- Limit fried items (vada, bajji, bonda) to once a week as a treat, not daily.
- Use a smaller plate for rice-based meals to naturally control portions.
- Fill half your plate with vegetables/salad, one-quarter with rice/millet, one-quarter with dal/curd.
- Avoid second helpings of rice; if still hungry, add more vegetables or curd instead.

---

## Sleep and Meal-Timing Advice

- Try to have dinner at least 2–3 hours before bedtime.
- Aim for 7–8 hours of sleep — poor sleep increases hunger and cravings the next day.
- Keep breakfast, lunch, and dinner at consistent times each day; this helps regulate hunger and digestion.
- Avoid skipping breakfast — it often leads to overeating later in the day.

## Simple Weekly Grocery List

**Grains & Millets**
- Rice (moderate quantity), idli/dosa rice or batter, ragi flour, millets (optional)

**Pulses & Lentils**
- Toor dal, moong dal, chana, sundal-friendly legumes (chickpeas, groundnuts)

**Vegetables** (rotate based on availability/price)
- Beans, cabbage, snake gourd, ladies finger, beetroot, drumstick, carrot, cucumber, greens (keerai/palak)

**Dairy**
- Curd, buttermilk, milk (low-fat)

**Others**
- Coconut (for chutney), tamarind, sambar powder, mustard seeds, curry leaves, jeera, methi seeds, lemons, seasonal fruits, roasted peanuts/chana, groundnut or gingelly oil
